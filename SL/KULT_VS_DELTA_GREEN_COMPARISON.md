#### DELTA GREEN 2025:

- **Hook:** Trip 19 crash (1940) → Volkov's interest → Mac Riley's obsession
- **Opening:** China Buffet meeting, team formation (5 player characters)
- **Antagonists:** Volkov mentioned, de tre männen unknown initially

  - Lovettsville crash site (Virginia)

**STORA SKILLNADER:**

- ✅ Real historical research integrated (Stanford, National Archives)

- ✅ Magda in Hamburg first, then Berlin (expanded geography)
- ❌ No immediate Berlin start

### CHAPTER 2: THE RITUAL

#### KULT ORIGINAL:
- **Setting:** Pogodin's mansion, Berlin
- **Time:** Night after Magda dies
- **Ritual:** De tre ryssarna (Anton, Sasha, Filip) summon Chagidiel's Incarnate
- **Mechanics:**
  - Three Russians are already Nepharites (Chagidiel's servants)
  - Ritual creates General Strelkov (Chagidiel's Incarnate)
  - Incarnate is NEW entity, physical manifestation of Death Angel
- **Outcome:**
  - General Strelkov appears, takes over Slava organization
  - Three Russians killed/consumed in ritual
  - Players witness horror, branded with Chagidiel's Mark
- **Mark effects:** Mental control, transformation begins, visible to others with Mark
- **Escape:** Players flee, now hunted by Incarnate's forces

#### DELTA GREEN 2025:
- **Setting:** Pogodin's mansion, Berlin
- **Time:** Night after Magda dies (samma timing)
- **Ritual:** De tre männen attempt to CREATE NEW INCARNATES
- **Mechanics:**
  - Anton, Sasha, Filip are 1942 survivors (NOT nepharites initially)
  - Ritual uses fragments from Leningrad + Camp S-17
  - Attempt: Summon THREE new incarnates (one each)
  - **CRITICAL FAILURE:** Dual manifestation instead
  - Yithian Echo attacks mentally (247Hz resonance)
  - Nyogtha manifests physically (Kalenko family transforms)
- **Outcome:**
  - **De tre männen SURVIVE** (flee Berlin, major change!)
  - Three transformerade (Katya, Yelena, Alyona Kalenko) appear
  - Filip Kramer dragged into darkness (survives but wounded)
  - Ritual BACKFIRES - didn't create controllable incarnates
- **Parasite exposure:**
  - Players exposed to fragment resonance
  - Hypergeometric parasite disease begins (6-stage progression)
  - Boils appear within days
- **Escape:** Players flee, de tre männen also flee, mutual chaos

**STORA SKILLNADER:**
- ✅ De tre männen LEVER (fortsätter som antagonister through campaign)
- ✅ Dual manifestation (Yithian + Nyogtha) istället för single Incarnate
- ✅ Ritual FAILURE theme (not successful summoning)
- ✅ Parasite disease mechanics (measurable 6-stage progression)
- ✅ Kalenko family transforms (emotional horror - children as monsters)
- ✅ 247Hz fragment resonance as weapon
- ❌ No single "General Strelkov" Incarnate figure
- ❌ No Mark (replaced with parasite disease)

  1. **Revelation:** Media connects SA to GG, de tre männen vanished

## SAMMANFATTNING: CORE PHILOSOPHY SHIFT

### KULT: "You Cannot Win"
- Archons control reality
- Corruption inevitable
- Escape = best outcome
- Cosmic pessimism

### DELTA GREEN 2025: "You Can Make A Difference"
- History can be understood
- Conspiracies can be exposed
- People can be saved
- Catastrophes can be prevented
- But at terrible personal cost

**The shift:** From nihilistic horror to heroic tragedy.

Players have agency, clear goals, and real stakes. They CAN win - but victory will hurt.
