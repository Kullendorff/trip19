MINI-SCENE 1: WERNER HARMANN NEGOTIATION (BERLIN)

Location: Abandoned Tempelhof warehouse, Berlin
Time: 2-3 hours before departure
Speltid: 30-45 minuter
Mood: Tense negotiation, last chance to back out

Werner's Appearance

  • Man in 60s, grått hår, sliten East German leather jacket (DDR-era)
  • Heavy smoker (chain-smoking Marlboro throughout meeting)
  • Missing two fingers on left hand (old smuggling accident)
  • Cold, professional demeanor - no warmth, no jokes
  • Eyes constantly scanning surroundings

Scene Start

Lastbilens motorn tickar när den svalnar i det kalla novembermörkret. Werner Harmann står lutad mot en rostig container, cigaretten glöder i mörkret. Han röker halvvägs innan han ens erkänner er närvaro.

Werner (på tyska, sen engelska): "So. Americans who want to disappear into Russia. Interesting times for such requests."

Han kastar cigaretten och trampar den med stöveln.

Werner: "Ivan Petrov waits in Warsaw. Good man. Drove for me since '09. You give him no trouble, he gives you no trouble. Understand?"

Route Overview (Werner's Briefing)

The Route

  • Path: "Warsaw → Minsk → Smolensk → Moscow. Four days if lucky. Six days if inspections."
  • Poland Checkpoint: "Easy. Belarus checkpoint is... uncomfortable. Smolensk checkpoint is where people die."
  • Container: "Modified cargo container. Air holes, water, bucket for... necessities."
  • Communication: "Ivan signals you when safe to move, when to stay frozen. You obey signals. Always."

Danger Points

  • Belarusian KGB: "Looks for smuggled weapons since Polen incident. They will search hard."
  • Smolensk FSB: "Military convoys everywhere. If they open container... pray they shoot quick."
  • Moscow Exit: "Textile factory area. Ivan knows safe building."

Payment & Legal

  • "You paid already through Slava's network. Good. Ivan knows to expect you."
  • "But if you are caught - I know nothing. Ivan knows nothing. You are cargo that loaded itself."

Final Warning

Werner lights another cigarette.

"Last year, three people tried this route. Two made it. One did not. We found him in Smolensk river."

Long pause. "Still want to go?"

Player Choices

CHOICE POINT: Last chance to reconsider or change routes

If players ask questions:

  • Werner answers professionally but reveals NO emotions
  • He will NOT discuss why they're going - "not my business"
  • He will NOT provide extra equipment - "you bring what you need"
  • He WILL confirm Ivan is reliable: "Ivan is best driver I know. You are safe as possible."

If players want to back out:

  • Werner shrugs: "Smart choice. But Slava will not return money."
  • They must choose different route (return to routes document)

If players want to negotiate:

  • Werner is DONE negotiating: "Price is set. Route is set. Yes or no?"
  • No Persuade checks work here - this is take-it-or-leave-it

Werner slaps the side of the truck twice.

Werner: "Ivan! They are coming!"

Werner går iväg utan att se sig om. Han är redan färdig med er.

HANDOUT: IVAN'S SIGNAL SYSTEM

Give to players when they enter container. They should write this down.

IVAN PETROV'S KNOCK SIGNALS

Learn these. Your life depends on understanding them.

Signal Meaning
THREE KNOCKS Stay absolutely quiet. Checkpoint approaching or inspection happening.
TWO KNOCKS Danger nearby but not immediate. Stay alert, minimize movement.
ONE KNOCK All clear. You can whisper, move carefully, use water.
RAPID KNOCKING (5+ fast) EMERGENCY: Abandon truck immediately. Exit rear and run.
No knocks for 6+ hours Something wrong. Ivan may be detained or killed.

YOUR SIGNALS TO IVAN:

  • Tap three times on wall: Medical emergency, need help
  • Tap SOS pattern (···---···): Extreme emergency, abort mission

SURVIVAL TIPS:

  • Ration water: Small sips every 2 hours
  • Use bucket behind privacy cloth
  • Stay calm: Panic uses oxygen and makes noise
  • Help teammates: If someone struggling, steady them
  • Trust Ivan: He has done this 50+ times

— Werner Harmann

CONSTITUTION CHECKS - SURVIVING THE CONTAINER

Players will make multiple Constitution checks throughout the journey. Track cumulative penalties.

Time in Container Check Failure Consequence
0-12 hours (Berlin → Warsaw) CON × 5, DC 40 -10% to all physical skills
12-24 hours (Warsaw → Belarus) CON × 5, DC varies by CON stat Lose 1d6 HP from exhaustion, dehydration
24-48 hours (Belarus → Smolensk) CON × 3, DC 60 (EXTREME) Fail silence check at Smolensk = discovery
48-60 hours (Smolensk → Moscow) CON × 5, DC 40 Collapse on arrival, need immediate rest

Cumulative Skill Penalties

  • After 24 hours: -10% to all physical skills
  • After 36 hours: -20% to all physical skills
  • After 48 hours: -30% to all physical skills
  • After 60 hours: -20% to ALL skills until 8 hours rest + food
These penalties should make players FEEL the ordeal. By Smolensk checkpoint, they should be desperate, exhausted, and terrified. This makes the climax appropriately tense.
