## "Through the Grey Waters"

**Campaign:** Trip 19 / Black Madonna - Chapter 5
**Route:** Route 1 (Cipher/Baltic Sea)
**Duration:** 3-4 days (variable depending on complications)
**Tone:** Paranoid thriller → claustrophobic tension → desperate arrival

**GM Options:**

## GEOPOLITICAL CONTEXT (November 2025)

**Critical to remember:**
- **Ukraina war:** 3.5 years ongoing, Russia isolated
- **Polen incident (July 2025):** Baltic borders on high alert
- **FSB surveillance:** Coast guard extremely active
- **Sankt Petersburg:** Putin's hometown = extra security
- **Western agents:** Hunted actively, torture if caught

**Players must understand:** This is NOT a movie. This is REAL danger.

## FULL VERSION BREAKDOWN

### MINI-SCEN 1: TALLINN PREPARATION
**Location:** Tallinn, Estonia
**Duration:** Day 1 (4-6 hours)
**Pacing:** Paranoid, hurried

#### Arrival in Tallinn

Players fly to Tallinn from Berlin/Frankfurt/Stockholm (commercial flights, legal entry to EU).

**Tallinn Airport:**
> "Lennart Meri Airport är litet, modernt, effektivt. Estniska gränsvakter kollar era pass snabbt - ni är EU-medborgare eller amerikaner med turistvisum. Inga problem. Men genom fönstren på andra sidan Finska viken - 80 kilometer bort - ligger Ryssland. Och det är dit ni ska."

**What players notice:**
- Estonian language (finno-ugric, incomprehensible)
- Mix of Soviet-era architecture and modern EU buildings
- NATO military presence (jets overhead occasionally)
- Many Finnish tourists (ferry connections)
- Tension in air - people know Russia is close

#### Hotel Check-in

**Recommended hotel:** Old Town Hostel or mid-range hotel
**Cover story:** Tourists visiting Baltic states

**Sparky's task:** Contact Cipher's smuggler connection via encrypted channels

**SIGINT Check (Sparky 80%):**
- Success: Contact established within 2-3 hours
- Failure: Takes 4-6 hours, raises digital footprint risk

**The Message (from Cipher):**
> "Contact: 'Dmitri'. 19:00 tonight. Balti Jaam Market, north entrance. He'll have red thermos. Password: 'Tänan sind'. He'll respond: 'Palun'. Bring 50% cash upfront ($50k). Good luck, Sparkplug."

#### Evening: The Meet

**Location:** Balti Jaam Market (indoor market near train station)
**Time:** 19:00, November darkness (sun sets 16:00 this latitude)
**Atmosphere:** Cold, crowded, mixed languages, steam from food stalls

**Finding Dmitri:**

> "Market är packad med folk efter jobbet. Doften av grillat kött, estnisk svartbröd, surkål. Vid norra ingången står en man, 40-tal, pälsmössa, röker, håller en röd termos. Hans ögon skannar folkmassan konstant. När ni närmar er spänner han sig."

**NPC: Dmitri (smuggler contact)**
- Age: 45, Estonian/Russian mix
- Background: Former merchant marine, now runs contraband
- Personality: Professional, paranoid, no small talk
- Motivation: Money, survival
- Languages: Estonian, Russian, broken English
- **Stats:** STR 12, DEX 10, INT 11, POW 13, Alertness 70%, Disguise 50%

**The Conversation (players must initiate):**

Player: "Tänan sind." (Thank you in Estonian)
Dmitri: "Palun." (Please/You're welcome) *studies them* "You have money?"

**Negotiation:**
- Dmitri wants $100k total (as established)
- $50k now, $50k on delivery to Sankt Petersburg
- Players can try to negotiate down (Persuade/Bargain check vs 50%)
- Success: $90k total
- Failure: He walks if they push too hard

**Information Dmitri provides (if paid):**

- "Tomorrow, 14:00. Port of Tallinn, Container Terminal A, Gate 3."
- "Bring only small bags. No electronics that track. Leave phones here or FSB will find you."
- "Crossing takes 8-12 hours depending on weather and patrols."
- "If Russian coast guard stops us, you hide. If they board, you stay silent or we all die."
- "Sankt Petersburg port is dangerous. I get you to dock, you disappear fast."

**Warning:**
> "Last month, FSB caught a boat. Four people. Nobody knows what happened to them. You sure you want to do this?"

Players can back out here. If they continue, Dmitri nods: "14:00. Don't be late."

#### Night Before Crossing

**What players can do:**
1. **Ditch electronics:** Leave phones/laptops at hotel (or burner phones only)
2. **Prepare gear:** Only essentials (weapons risk, must hide)
3. **Rest:** Constitution checks if they don't (exhaustion next day)
4. **Research:** Learn basic Russian phrases, Sankt Petersburg maps
5. **Bonds:** Call loved ones? (knowing it might be last time)

**Parasite disease check:**
- Stage 2 symptoms continue: boils spread, larvae movement, itching
- 247Hz humming present if near fragments/Doll
- Players weaken slightly (CON checks harder)

**SAN-loss:** 0/1 (anticipation of extreme danger)

### MINI-SCEN 2: THE SEA CROSSING
**Location:** Tallinn Port → Finska viken → Sankt Petersburg
**Duration:** Day 2 (8-12 hours, depending on complications)
**Pacing:** Claustrophobic → tension → potential action

#### Boarding the Vessel

**14:00, Container Terminal A, Gate 3:**

> "Tallinn hamn är industriell, kall, cementgrå. Containrar staplade som lego-block. Truckar kör förbi med avgaser. Dmitri väntar vid Gate 3, röker, gestikulerar: 'Kom. Snabbt.'"

**The Ship:** MV *Põhjatäht* (North Star)
- Small cargo vessel, ~40 meters, Estonian flag
- Carries "legitimate" cargo: machinery parts, industrial supplies
- Crew: 4 (captain + 3 crew, all Estonian/Russian mix)
- Age: 30+ years old, rusty but functional

**Hiding Place:**

> "Dmitri leder er ombord via lastrampen. Under däck, bakom staplade containrar, finns ett gömt utrymme - 3x4 meter, ingen ventilation, luktar diesel och salt. 'Here,' säger Dmitri. 'Stay quiet. No light. No sound. I bring water, food. We leave in one hour.'"

**What's in the hiding space:**
- Blankets (old, damp)
- Water bottles (6)
- Dried food (bread, sausage, chocolate)
- Bucket (for waste - grim reality)
- No light (flashlights risk detection)
- Cold (November Baltic = near freezing)

**Departure:** 15:00, vessel leaves Tallinn

**Constitution Check (all players):**
- Success: Handle claustrophobia, cold, darkness
- Failure: Nausea, anxiety, -10% to all checks during crossing

---

#### The Crossing: Hour-by-Hour

**OPTION A: UNEVENTFUL CROSSING (Fast-forward)**

> "Åtta timmar i mörker och kyla. Skeppet gungar. Motorn dånar konstant. Ni hör besättningen röra sig ovanför, ryska röster, metallskrammel. Ingen kommer ner. Vid 23:00 stannar motorn. Ni är framme."

**Constitution checks:** Each player rolls
- Success: Exhausted but functional
- Failure: -10% all checks next 12 hours, nausea

**OPTION B: TENSION-FILLED CROSSING (Play out events)**

Pick 1-3 events from complication table below, or roll 1d10 for each 3-hour segment:

**HOUR 0-3: Departure & Open Water**

> "Skeppet lämnar hamnen. Genom väggen hör ni Tallinn försvinna. Motorn blir högre när ni når öppet vatten. Finska viken är sträv - vågor slår mot skrovet. Ni kastas mot väggar."

- Seasickness checks (CON rolls)
- Players can't see each other (total darkness)
- Sound of water constant, overwhelming
- 247Hz humming (if marked players) mixes with engine noise

**HOUR 3-6: Mid-Crossing**

*Roll 1d10 or choose:*

| Roll | Event |
|------|-------|
| 1-3 | Routine crossing (no event) |
| 4-5 | Weather worsens (storm) |
| 6-7 | Patrol boat approaches |
| 8 | Crew argument (heard above) |
| 9 | Hidden cargo issue (fragment exposure?) |
| 10 | FSB BOARDING (critical event) |

**Event: Weather Storm (4-5)**

> "Vågorna ökar. Skeppet krän violently. Vatten sipprar genom väggarna. Temperaturen sjunker. Ni kastas mot varandra i mörkret."

- Constitution checks (all players)
- Failure: 1D3 HP damage (bruising), nausea
- Crossing delayed +2 hours
- Risk: Cargo shifts, could expose hiding place

**Event: Patrol Boat Approaches (6-7)**

> "Motorn saktar. Genom väggen: ryska röster. Radio knastr. Dmitri ropar på ryska: 'Kapten! Паруль!' (Patrol!). Skeppet stannar."

**What happens:**
- Russian coast guard patrol boat pulls alongside
- Crew must show papers, cargo manifest
- Players hear Russian voices, boots on deck
- Must stay ABSOLUTELY SILENT (Alertness checks to avoid noise)

**Alertness Checks (all players, opposed by Coast Guard Alertness 60%):**
- All success: Undetected
- One failure: Coast guard suspicious, searches more (second check)
- Two failures: Coast guard hears noise, searches hiding area (CRITICAL)

**If detected (CRITICAL ENCOUNTER):**
- Coast guard demands to search
- Dmitri tries to bribe (Persuade check 50%)
  - Success: Guards take bribe ($10k), leave
  - Failure: Guards insist on boarding

**If guards board:**
Players have 30 seconds to decide:
1. **Hide better** (Stealth checks, HARD)
2. **Surrender** (arrested, taken to FSB facility - campaign over?)
3. **Fight** (4 coast guards, armed with AK-12s - lethal encounter)
4. **Diplomatic** (Russian speaker tries to talk out, VERY HARD)

**Combat Stats (Russian Coast Guard x4):**
- STR 13, DEX 11, CON 12, INT 10, POW 10
- HP: 12 each
- Armor: Tactical vest (3 armor)
- Weapons: AK-12 (1d12 damage), pistols (1d10)
- Skills: Firearms 60%, Alertness 60%, Hand-to-Hand 50%

**If combat occurs:**
- Loud, chaotic, lethal
- Crew might help players (or betray them)
- Patrol boat calls for backup (10-15 minutes away)
- Players must kill all guards + escape before backup arrives
- **SAN-loss:** 1/1D6 (killing coast guards = international incident, moral weight)
- **Consequences:** FSB will hunt them specifically in Sankt Petersburg

**Event: FSB Boarding (10) - WORST CASE**

> "Motorn stannar abrupt. Boots på däck - många. Ryska röster, auktoritära. 'ФСБ! Приготовиться к досмотру!' (FSB! Prepare for inspection!)"

This is critical - FSB doesn't take bribes as easily.

**Dmitri panics (heard through walls):**
"ФСБ... блядь... они знают." (FSB... fuck... they know.)

**Players have 1 minute to decide:**
- **Option A:** Perfect hiding (Stealth checks OPPOSED by FSB 80% - very hard)
- **Option B:** Disguise as crew (requires planning earlier + Russian language)
- **Option C:** Surrender (arrested, interrogated - might survive with trauma)
- **Option D:** Sabotage ship (create distraction - fire, engine failure)
- **Option E:** Jump overboard (freezing water, 5km from shore, certain death)

**If caught by FSB:**
- Interrogated on patrol boat
- Accused of espionage
- Taken to Sankt Petersburg FSB facility
- Torture, imprisonment, or execution likely
- Campaign can continue (rescue mission?) but fundamentally changed

**HOUR 6-9: Approach to Russian Waters**

> "Motorn sänker farten. Genom skrovet känner ni vibrationerna ändras - ni närmar er hamn. Dmitri kommer ner med ficklampa: 'Vi är nära. Snart inne.'"

- Players can finally see each other (exhausted, pale, cold)
- Dmitri warns: "Sankt Petersburg port is FSB everywhere. I dock at industrial area, not main harbor. You have 5 minutes to get off, disappear."
- He gives directions to nearest metro station (20-minute walk)

**Constitution checks (final):**
- Success: Functional
- Failure: Exhausted (-10% all checks for 12 hours)

**HOUR 9-12: Sankt Petersburg Harbor Arrival**

> "23:00. Skeppet dockar i industrihamn. Genom väggarna: ryska röster, truck-motorer, metal-ljud. Dmitri gestikulerar: 'Nu. Gå. Snabbt.'"

### MINI-SCEN 3: SANKT PETERSBURG ARRIVAL & ESCAPE
**Location:** Industrial harbor district, Sankt Petersburg
**Duration:** 30-60 minutes (critical window)
**Pacing:** Desperate, paranoid

#### Disembarkation

> "Ni kliver ut på rysk mark för första gången. Kall novembernatt, -5°C. Industrihamnen är mörk, bara stränga lampor vid kranar. Containrar staplade som labyrinter. I fjärran: Sankt Petersburg lights, spire av Peter-Paul fortress, city glow."

**What players see:**
- Industrial zone, 3km from city center
- Harbor workers (Russian, ignore foreigners - suspicion)
- Occasional FSB patrols (black vans with spotlights)
- Signs in Cyrillic only
- Smell: diesel, salt water, industrial chemicals

**Dmitri's farewell:**

> "Dmitri pekar österut: 'Metro station - Primorskaya - 20 minutes walk. Follow road, don't talk to anyone. FSB patrol every 30 minutes here.' Han håller fram hand: 'Balance. $50k. Now.'"

Players must pay remaining $50k (or deal with consequences).

**If they try to stiff him:**
- Dmitri: "You fuck me, I call FSB right now. You choose."
- He has radio, will report them if betrayed
- Not worth the risk

**If they pay:**
- Dmitri nods, disappears into darkness
- They never see him again
- (GM note: He survives if players succeeded, dies if FSB caught them earlier)

#### The Walk to Metro

**20-minute walk through industrial district:**

**Stealth/Alertness checks (group check):**
- Success: Reach metro without incident
- Failure: Encounter complication (see below)

**Encounter Table (roll 1d10 if failed):**

| Roll | Encounter |
|------|-----------|
| 1-3 | **Harbor Worker:** Russian asks "Вы кто?" (Who are you?) - must bluff or run |
| 4-5 | **FSB Patrol Van:** Spotlights sweep area - must hide (Stealth checks) |
| 6-7 | **Drunk Locals:** Group of drunk Russians, potentially violent or helpful (random) |
| 8 | **Stray Dogs:** Pack of aggressive strays (5-6 dogs), can be dangerous |
| 9 | **Police Checkpoint:** Must detour (+15 minutes), risk missing last metro |
| 10 | **FSB DIRECT ENCOUNTER:** Stopped and questioned (CRITICAL) |

**CRITICAL: FSB Direct Encounter**

> "Черный фургон останавливается рядом с вами. Двое людей выходят - штатское, но явно ФСБ. 'Стоп. Документы.' (Stop. Documents.)"

Players must decide:
1. **Show fake IDs** (if they have any - HARD Disguise check)
2. **Run** (chase scene, can work but escalates situation)
3. **Fight** (2 FSB agents, armed - lethal, brings backup)
4. **Surrender** (arrested, interrogated - campaign pivot)

**If they run (Chase Scene):**

- Players have 30 seconds head start
- FSB agents call backup (3-5 minutes away)
- Must reach metro and board train before caught
- Navigation checks (INT rolls) to find route
- Athletics checks (DEX/CON) to maintain speed
- If caught: Combat or surrender

#### Primorskaya Metro Station

**If they reach the metro:**

> "Primorskaya station - Soviet brutalism i betong och stål. Flurescerande ljus, folk i vinterjackor, silence. Ni ser ut som skit - våta, utmattade, desperate. Folk stirrar."

**Problems:**
1. **Tickets:** Need Russian rubles (can buy at machine - Sparky's Russian 0% = struggle)
2. **Attention:** Look foreign, exhausted, suspicious
3. **Surveillance:** Cameras everywhere
4. **Language:** Signs in Cyrillic only

**Buying Tickets:**

- Machines accept cash only (rubles)
- Players must have exchanged money earlier OR use credit card (leaves trail)
- Can ask Russian for help (INT/CHA check to communicate without language)

**Platform:**

> "Ni går ner till perrongen. Kakelväggarna är smaragdgröna, Soviet-æstetik. Ljudet av tåg ekoe genom tunnlar. 247Hz-hummingen känns starkare här - något under staden?"

**Next train:** 5-10 minutes (depending on time)
**Destination:** Moskovsky vokzal (train station to Moscow)
**Duration:** 30 minutes metro ride

**Complication (optional):**
- FSB agents enter metro behind them (if earlier encounter)
- Must blend in on train, avoid eye contact
- Stealth checks to avoid detection

### MINI-SCEN 4: RED ARROW DEPARTURE
**Location:** Moskovsky vokzal (Saint Petersburg railway station)
**Duration:** 30-60 minutes
**Pacing:** Final tension → relief (temporary)

#### Arrival at Moskovsky Vokzal

**00:30-01:00 (midnight to 1 AM):**

> "Moskovsky vokzal är en massiv gul building från 1851, tsaristisk arkitektur. Inne: kaos av resande trots natten. Avgångstavlan på ryska och engelska: 'Красная стрела / Red Arrow - Moskva - 01:55 - Peron 3.'"

**The Red Arrow:**
- Legendary night train (Saint Petersburg → Moscow)
- 8-hour journey (arrives Moscow 10:00 AM)
- Sleeper cars available (expensive but essential)
- Many businessmen, families, tourists (some)

**Buying Tickets:**

**Challenge:** Ticket counter only Russian speakers

**Options:**
1. **Sparky attempts Russian (0%):** Fumbles, but eventually buys (takes 20 minutes, embarrassing)
2. **Find English-speaking helper:** Persuade check, find tourist/student who helps
3. **Ticket machine:** Easier, accepts cards, has English option

**Cost:** ~8,000 rubles per person (~$80) for sleeper car

**Security Check:**

> "Before boarding: metal detectors, bag x-rays, passport check. Russian police (MVD) check everyone."

**Passport Control:**
- Players must show IDs (fake or real)
- If fake IDs: Disguise/Forgery check (vs Police Alertness 50%)
- Police suspicious of Western foreigners but not aggressive (yet)
- Risk: If FSB alerted earlier, they might be flagged

**Boarding:**

> "Ni boardar tåget - gröna vagnar med gulddetaljer. Sovkupéer är små: 4 bunks per compartment, shared with strangers eller private (extra cost). Lukten: tea, cigarettes (illegal but smoked anyway), old upholstery."

**Departure: 01:55 sharp**

> "Tåget lämnar Sankt Petersburg i mörkret. Genom fönstret: stadens ljus försvinner, replaced by oändlig rysk skog. Ni har gjort det. Ni är i Ryssland."

**What happens on train:** (See CHAPTER5_STRUCTURE.md Scene 8 for full train scene)

**Summary:**
- Players finally rest (8 hours)
- Nightmares about Leningrad 1942 (SAN 0/1)
- Parasite symptoms worsen
- Black Madonna Doll huttar constantly
- Arrival Moscow 10:00 AM → Proceed to Scene 1

## GM NOTES

**Pacing advice:**
- **If players need action:** Use Encounter A or B (combat/chase)
- **If players exhausted:** Fast-forward crossing, focus on arrival tension
- **If time limited:** Fast-forward entire scene (15 minutes)

**Tone:**
- NOT action movie (James Bond)
- Paranoid thriller (Tinker Tailor Soldier Spy)
- Real danger, consequences matter
- Players should feel scared, not heroic

**Geopolitical weight:**
- Emphasize Ukraina war context
- FSB is not cartoon villain - professional, dangerous
- If caught, likely torture/execution
- No cavalry coming to save them

**Player agency:**
- Let them plan, make choices
- Reward clever thinking
- Punish recklessness (but not unfairly)
- No "correct" path - multiple solutions valid
