### MINI-SCENE 1: WERNER HARMANN NEGOTIATION (BERLIN)

**Location:** Abandoned Tempelhof warehouse, Berlin (2-3 hours before departure)
**Speltid:** 30-45 minuter
**Mood:** Tense negotiation, last chance to back out

#### Setup

Werner Harmann möter agenternas lastbil vid ett övergivet lagerhus i Tempelhof. Han är en man i 60-årsåldern, grått hår, sliten skinnjacka, rökande Marlboro. Talar brytande engelska med tjock tysk accent.

**Werner's Appearance:**
- Sliten East German leather jacket (DDR-era)
- Heavy smoker (chain-smoking throughout meeting)
- Missing two fingers on left hand (old smuggling accident)
- Cold, professional demeanor - no warmth, no jokes
- Eyes constantly scanning surroundings

#### Scene Start

> *Lastbilens motorn tickar när den svalnar i det kalla novembermörkret. Werner Harmann står lutad mot en rostig container, cigaretten glöder i mörkret. Han röker halvvägs innan han ens erkänner er närvaro.*
>
> **Werner (på tyska, sen engelska):** "So. Americans who want to disappear into Russia. Interesting times for such requests."
>
> *Han kastar cigaretten och trampar den med stöveln.*
>
> **Werner:** "Ivan Petrov waits in Warsaw. Good man. Drove for me since '09. You give him no trouble, he gives you no trouble. Understand?"

#### Key Information Werner Provides

**Route Overview:**
- "Warsaw → Minsk → Smolensk → Moscow. Four days if lucky. Six days if inspections."
- "Poland checkpoint is easy. Belarus checkpoint is... uncomfortable. Smolensk checkpoint is where people die."
- "You hide in modified cargo container. Air holes, water, bucket for... necessities."
- "Ivan signals you when safe to move, when to stay frozen. You obey signals. Always."

**Danger Points:**
- "Belarusian KGB looks for smuggled weapons since Polen incident. They will search hard."
- "Smolensk is FSB territory. Military convoys everywhere. If they open container... pray they shoot quick."
- "In Moscow, you exit at industrial district. Textile factory area. Ivan knows safe building."

**Payment:**
- "You paid already through Slava's network. Good. Ivan knows to expect you."
- "But if you are caught - I know nothing. Ivan knows nothing. You are cargo that loaded itself."

**Final Warning:**
- "Last year, three people tried this route. Two made it. One did not. We found him in Smolensk river."
- *Werner lights another cigarette.* "Still want to go?"

#### Player Choices

**CHOICE POINT:** Last chance to reconsider or change routes

**If players ask questions:**
- Werner answers professionally but reveals no emotions
- He will NOT discuss why they're going - "not my business"
- He will NOT provide extra equipment - "you bring what you need"
- He WILL confirm Ivan is reliable: "Ivan is best driver I know. You are safe as possible."

**If players want to back out:**
- Werner shrugs: "Smart choice. But Slava will not return money."
- They must choose different route (back to RUSSIA_INFILTRATION_ROUTES.md)

**If players want to negotiate:**
- Werner is DONE negotiating: "Price is set. Route is set. Yes or no?"
- No Persuade checks work here - this is take-it-or-leave-it

#### Transition to Scene

> *Werner slaps the side of the truck twice.*
>
> **Werner:** "Ivan! They are coming!"
>
> *En tjock man i 50-årsåldern klättrar ur förarhytten. Detta är Ivan Petrov - rysk lastbilsförare med enorm buk, kalufsmössa, och ovårdat skägg. Han säger ingenting, bara nickar kort.*
>
> **Werner:** "Get in container. Ivan leaves in ten minutes. Good luck."
>
> *Werner går iväg utan att se sig om. Han är redan färdig med er.*

**Skill Checks (Optional):**
- **Sense Motive (HUMINT) vs Werner's Deception:** DC 50
  - Success: Werner is genuinely trying to help (in his own cold way) - this isn't a setup
- **Sense Motive (HUMINT) vs Ivan's... nothing:** DC impossible
  - Ivan is completely unreadable - neutral professional face at all times

**SAN Loss:** None (yet)

### MINI-SCENE 2: TRUCK LOADING & CONTAINER LIFE (BERLIN → WARSAW)

**Duration:** 12-14 hours drive
**Speltid:** 20-30 minuter OR fast-forward
**Mood:** Claustrophobic, uncertain, building dread

#### The Container

Agenterna klättrar in i en modifierad fraktcontainer på Ivan's lastbil:

**Container Features:**
- 20-foot shipping container (6m x 2.4m x 2.4m)
- Modified false wall creates hidden compartment (3m x 2.4m x 2.4m)
- Air holes drilled in strategic locations (hidden from outside inspection)
- Four sleeping mats, six water bottles, bucket for waste
- Small LED lantern (battery powered, dim red light)
- NO phones, NO communication with outside once sealed

**Container Conditions:**
- Temperature: ~5°C (November cold)
- Light: Dim red LED only (to preserve night vision for quick exits)
- Sound: Muffled road noise, can hear Ivan's radio faintly
- Movement: Every bump and turn felt directly
- Duration: 12-14 hours to Warsaw, with only brief stops

#### Sealing In

> *Ivan guidar er in i containern med en ficklampa. Han pekar på LED-lampan, vattnen, hinken. Säger ingenting. Sen slår han hårt tre gånger på metallväggen:*
>
> **Ivan (ryska, tjock accent):** "Three knocks: stay quiet. Two knocks: checkpoint coming. One knock: all clear."
>
> *Han börjar svetsa igen den falska väggen.*
>
> **Ivan:** "No light during checkpoint. No sound. No movement. Understand?"
>
> *Sen är dörren stängd. Svetsljuset blinkar utanför. Sen mörker.*

#### The First Hours (Berlin → Polish Border)

**GM beskrivning:**

> *De första timmarna är ett experiment i diskomfort. Containern är kall, metallväggar svettas kondens i mörkret. Varje grop i vägen känns som en bomb. Det luktar metall, olja, och gammalt trä.*
>
> *Efter två timmar: Truck driver stannar. Ni hör röster utanför - tyska. Bensinstation? Ivan tankar, sedan vidare.*
>
> *Efter fyra timmar: Ivan knackar tre gånger hårt. STAY QUIET. Truck driver saktar in. Ni hör... polska röster? Checkpoint?*

**CHECKPOINT #1: POLISH BORDER (Friendly)**

**Skill Checks:**
- **Stealth (Group Check):** DC 30 (easy - Polish guards not looking for people)
  - Failure: Guards hear movement, ask Ivan to open container
  - Success: Guards wave truck through after checking documents

**If Guards Inspect:**
- Ivan opens FRONT section of container (false wall hides agenterna)
- Guards see "textile shipments" (fake cargo Werner prepared)
- **Ivan's Deception vs Guard's Sense Motive:** Auto-success (Ivan rolls ~60%)
- Guards satisfied, truck continues

**Polish Guard Dialogue (if opened):**
> **Guard (polska):** "What are you carrying?"
> **Ivan (polska, bored tone):** "Textiles. Moscow delivery. Same shit as last month."
> **Guard:** "Open it."
> *Ivan öppnar fram-sektionen. Guards checks manifest, shines flashlight, satisfied.*
> **Guard:** "Go. Move along."

**After Polish Border:**
- Ivan knackar en gång: ALL CLEAR
- Agenterna can breathe again
- **Con × 5 check:** DC varies by agent's CON
  - Failure: Starting to feel cramped, dehydrated, uncomfortable (-10% to physical skills)
  - Success: Managing okay so far

#### Warsaw Stop (Brief)

> *Efter ytterligare åtta timmar stannar truck driver igen. Ni hör polska stadsljud - bilar, röster, liv. Ivan öppnar containern en spricka och skjuter in två plastpåsar:*
>
> **Ivan (ryska):** "Fifteen minutes. Food. Stretch. Then we go Belarus."
>
> *Påsarna innehåller: Polski kielbasa sandwiches, äpplen, två liter vatten, fyra Red Bulls.*

**Brief Respite:**
- 15 minuter att äta, sträcka på benen (inte lämna container), använda hinken bakom presenning
- Ivan står guard utanför, rökande, ensuring ingen ser
- **Medicine or Survival check:** DC 40
  - Success: Advise group on staying hydrated, preventing cramps for next leg
  - Provides +20% bonus to next Constitution checks

**Optional Roleplay:**
- Agenterna can talk quietly here (first chance since Berlin)
- Discuss plan, reassure each other, make peace with choices
- This is LAST comfortable moment before extreme danger

#### Transition

>
> **Ivan:** "Next stop: Belarus. Harder border. Stay quiet no matter what. Understand?"
>
> *Han stänger dörren igen. Svetsen blinkar till. Lastbilen startar.*
>
> *Ni vet att nästa checkpoint är BELARUS - och det är ingen vän till USA.*

**Speltid:** 45-60 minuter (this is the TENSE one)
**Mood:** Peak tension, possible disaster

**CHECKPOINT #2: BELARUS-POLAND BORDER (Hostile)**

**Situation:**
- Belarusian KGB checkpoint (not regular border guards)
- Polen incident (July 2025) has made this border EXTREMELY hostile
- They are looking for weapons smuggling, NATO equipment, saboteurs
- Ivan has done this 50+ times but it's NEVER routine

#### The Inspection (Skill Challenge)

**ROUND 1: Document Check**

> **Belarusian Guard (ryska, aggressiv ton):** "Documents. Manifest. What cargo?"
>
> **Ivan (ryska, bored professional):** "Textiles. Moscow delivery. Regular route."
>
> **Guard:** "You were in Poland four hours? Why so long?"
>
> **Ivan:** "Traffic. Warsaw construction. Same as always."

**Ivan's Deception vs Guard's Sense Motive:**
- Ivan rolls Deception: 65%
- Guards roll Sense Motive: 40%
- Ivan wins (normal outcome)

**If Guards become suspicious (10% chance or GM fiat):**
- "Open the container. We inspect."

**ROUND 2: Container Inspection (IF TRIGGERED)**

> *Ni hör guards walking around truck. Boots crunching gravel. Flashlights clicking on.*
>
> *METAL CLANG - någon slår på container-dörren med batong.*
>
> **Guard:** "Open it!"
>
> *Ivan's keys jingle. Lock turns. Doors swing open.*

**GROUP STEALTH CHECK:** All agenterna roll Stealth
- **DC 40** (Belarusian guards are LOOKING for smuggled people after Polen incident)
- **Modifiers:**
  - Constitution < 10: -10% (exhausted, might cough/move)
  - Constitution 10-12: +0%
  - Constitution 13+: +10% (managing discomfort well)

**SUCCESS (All pass):**
- Guards shine flashlights into front section
- See textile cargo (false wall holds)
- Satisfied after 2-3 minutes of inspection
- Wave truck through

**FAILURE (Anyone fails):**
- Guard hears movement/breathing in hidden section
- "Wait. What was that sound?"
- **CRISIS POINT** (see Complication #9: "Heard in Container")

**ROUND 3: Drug/Weapons Check (Random, 30% chance)**

> **Guard:** "Wait. We check for drugs. Bring the dog."
>
> *Ni hör hundskall kommer närmare. Shepherd dog, trained to detect drugs, explosives, and... people.*

**Situation:**
- German Shepherd trained to detect humans
- Will likely alert on hidden compartment
- This is a 1d100 roll: 01-40 = dog alerts, 41-100 = dog doesn't detect

**If Dog Alerts:**
- Guards WILL open hidden compartment
- Agenterna are CAUGHT
- See "Disaster Scenario: Caught at Belarus Border" below

**If Dog Doesn't Alert (GM mercy or lucky roll):**
- Dog sniffs container, seems interested, but handler pulls it away
- "Probably just rats. Or textile chemicals. Move on."
- Truck continues

#### Disaster Scenario: CAUGHT AT BELARUS BORDER

**If agenterna are discovered:**

> *Ljuset bländar er när guards river upp den falska väggen. Fyra Belarusian KGB agents med AK-74s riktar vapen mot er.*
>
> **Lead Guard (ryska, skriker):** "OUT! HANDS UP! NOW!"

**Agenterna have THREE OPTIONS:**

**OPTION 1: SURRENDER**
- Agenterna put hands up, surrender peacefully
- They are arrested, separated, interrogated
- **CAMPAIGN OVER** (or massive rescue operation required)
- KGB will discover they are Delta Green operatives
- International incident, possible execution

**OPTION 2: FIGHT**
- Combat begins (see "Optional Action Encounter: Belarus Border Fight")
- 4 KGB agents + 6 backup within 30 seconds
- Even if agenterna win, they are now WANTED by Belarus/Russia
- Must flee back to Poland (Scene 0B FAILS, choose different route)

**OPTION 3: BRIBE (Desperate)**
- **Persuade check at -40%** (KGB agents are ideological, not corrupt)
- Offering $50,000+ USD might work (DC 60 even with bribe)
- **Success:** Guards take money, let you go with WARNING
  - "If we see you again, we shoot first."
  - Ivan is furious but continues route
- **Failure:** Guards take money AND arrest you
  - "Thank you for donation to Belarus security services. Now, hands up."

**OPTION 4: RUN**
- Agenterna bolt from container, try to escape into Polish side
- **Athletics check:** DC 50 to outrun guards
- **Firearms check (Guards):** 60% to hit fleeing agents
- If anyone is hit, they are captured or killed
- If all escape, they must return to Poland (Scene 0B FAILS)

**GM NOTE:** This should feel like DISASTER if caught. Belarus is not friendly. This is real danger.

**Situation:**
- This is Russia's "western front" security checkpoint
- FSB Counter-Intelligence Division operates here (not regular border guards)
- They are looking for: NATO agents, saboteurs, Ukrainian intelligence
- Since Ukraine war: 300% increase in inspections
- Since Polen incident: paranoia at maximum levels

**Why Smolensk Is So Dangerous:**
- Putin's symbolic "gateway to Motherland"
- 150km from Moscow - last chance to catch infiltrators
- M1 highway carries military supplies to Ukraine front
- FSB knows smuggling routes exist and ACTIVELY hunts them
- Ivan has lost colleagues here before

#### The Wait (Building Dread)

> *Truck driver står still i kö. Ni hör andra trucks framför er getting inspected. Dogs barking. Metal detectors beeping. Shouts in Russian.*
>
> *After thirty minutes: Truck driver rullar framåt 20 meters. Stannar igen.*
>

>

**Agenterna must stay COMPLETELY SILENT for 2-3 hours:**

**Constitution × 3 Check (EXTREME):**
- **DC 60** (this is brutal - cramped, terrified, exhausted, need to pee)

  - Each agent can help ONE other agent (+20% bonus) by steadying them

**SUCCESS (All pass):**

**FAILURE (Anyone fails):**
- Agent coughs, shifts weight, drops water bottle, etc.

#### FSB Inspection (Standard Procedure)

**Ivan's interaction with FSB:**

> **FSB Agent (ryska, cold professional):** "Documents. Destination. Purpose."
>
> **Ivan (ryska, tired worker tone):** "Textile delivery. Moscow Tekstilshchiki district. Regular run."
>
> **FSB Agent:** "You came through Belarus? Why not direct route?"
>
> **Ivan:** "Cheaper fuel in Minsk. Company orders."
>
> **FSB Agent:** "Wait here. We inspect."

**INSPECTION STAGES:**

**Stage 1: Visual Inspection**
- FSB agent walks around truck with flashlight
- Checks tire wells (common hiding spot)
- Checks undercarriage with mirror-on-stick
- Looks for fresh welding marks (CONCEALMENT CHECK)

**Ivan's Concealment (False Wall) vs FSB Alertness:**
- Ivan's Concealment: 70% (he's very good)
- FSB Alertness: 50% (trained but not expecting THIS truck)
- **Roll:** Ivan usually succeeds (70% > 50%)

**If FSB notices fresh welding:**
- "These welding marks are new. Open the container."
- Proceed to Stage 2

**Stage 2: Container Opening (60% chance)**
- FSB opens front section
- Inspects textile cargo with flashlight
- Checks manifest against cargo
- **Ivan's Deception vs FSB Sense Motive:**
  - Ivan: 65%
  - FSB: 55% (they are GOOD)
  - Close call every time

**If FSB is satisfied:**
- "Okay. Move along."
- Skip to Success Scenario

**If FSB is suspicious:**
- Proceed to Stage 3

**Stage 3: Deep Inspection (30% chance)**
- FSB brings drug dog and metal detector
- Scans container with portable X-ray (looking for weapons)
- Dog sniffs for explosives/humans

**Metal Detector Check:**
- Will detect: guns, knives, electronics
- Agenterna must have ditched phones/weapons OR hidden in shielded bags
- **If agents kept weapons/phones:** Detector alerts → CAUGHT

**Drug Dog Check:**
- Same as Belarus border: 1d100
- 01-50 = Dog alerts on human scent
- 51-100 = Dog doesn't alert (cargo smell masks humans)

**X-Ray Check (Rare, 10% chance):**
- Portable X-ray scans container
- WILL reveal hidden compartment and people
- If this happens → CAUGHT (no escape)
