📍 Key areas for your stay

HafenCity

Modern harbor district. Walking distance to Überseequartier and Elbphilharmonie. Trendy, expensive, excellent transport.

Altstadt / City Center

Central, near Hauptbahnhof. Best transport connections. Mix of business and tourist hotels. Easy access everywhere.

St. Pauli

Lively, budget-friendly. Famous nightlife district. 15 min to HafenCity by U-Bahn. Good value, less conspicuous.
