GEOPOLITISK KONTEXT (2025)

KRITISKT att ha i åtanke:

Sankt Petersburg specialstatus

  • Putins hemstad: Extra FSB-närvaro, konstant övervakning
  • Flottbas: Östersjöflottans högkvarter i närheten
  • Ukrainakrigets påverkan: Hamnen används för militär logistik
  • Västerländska sanktioner: Smugglingsnätverk aktiva men farliga

Östersjöförhållanden (november 2025)

  • Temperatur: Luft ~0-5°C, Vatten ~5-8°C (risk för hypotermi)
  • Väder: Stormar vanliga, grova hav, begränsad sikt
  • NATO-närvaro: Estniska vatten hårt övervakade
  • Ryska patrulleringar: Kustbevakningen aktiv, letar efter NATO-aktivitet

Red Arrow tåget (Sankt Petersburg → Moskva)

  • Berömd rutt: Historiskt natttåg, lyxvagnar
  • FSB-övervakning: Känd rutt för utländska resenärer
  • Täckhistoria: Affärsresenärer, turister (riskabelt men trovärdigt)
  • Varaktighet: 8 timmar över natten, anländer Moskva på morgonen

MINI-SCEN 1: TALLINN FÖRBEREDELSE

Plats: Tallinn hamndistrikt, övergiven lagerlokal
Speltid: 30-45 minuter
Stämning: Kall, spänd, the point of no return

Scen start

Tallinn harbor district luktar salt, diesel, och ruttna fiskar. November-vinden skär genom kläder som knivar. Ni står utanför ett övergivet lagerhus, Baltiska havet svart och ilsket i bakgrunden.

En man i sliten sjömansjacka väntar vid en rostig ståldörr. Detta är Dmitri - Sparky's kontakt via darkweb-forumet. Han röker filterless cigarett och säger ingenting.

Dmitri (ryska, sen engelska): "You are late. Boat leaves in one hour. Sea is angry tonight. Good for hiding from patrols, bad for surviving."

Han öppnar dörren. Inside: en liten fishing trawler, 40 fot lång, rutten men sjöduglig.

Dmitri's Briefing

Route Overview

  • Departure: Tonight, 23:00, no lights, no radio contact
  • Route: Tallinn → Gulf of Finland → Sankt Petersburg harbor (200 nautical miles)
  • Duration: 36-48 hours depending on weather and patrols
  • Cover Story: "Lost fishing vessel" if intercepted (weak cover)

Danger Points

  • Estonian Coast Guard: Will stop boat if seen leaving illegally
  • Russian Coast Guard: Armed patrols, shoot first mentality since Ukraine war
  • Hypothermia: November Baltic = 5-8°C water temperature, death in 30 minutes if overboard
  • Sankt Petersburg Entry: Must arrive at night, avoid harbor patrol, dock at abandoned pier

What Dmitri Provides

  • 40-foot fishing trawler (disguised as Estonian vessel)
  • Warm clothes, blankets, emergency rations
  • Hidden compartment below deck (4 people, cramped)
  • Contact info for Sankt Petersburg safe house (if asked)

Payment

Already paid through Cipher's network ($50,000-70,000 total). Dmitri will NOT negotiate further.

Player Choices

CHOICE POINT: Last chance to reconsider or prepare

If players ask questions:

  • Dmitri answers factually but reveals NO emotions
  • He will NOT discuss why they're going - "not my business"
  • He WILL confirm route is survivable: "I do this twelve times. You will be thirteenth."

If players want to back out:

  • Dmitri shrugs: "Your choice. But Cipher will not return money."
  • Must choose different route (back to RUSSIA_INFILTRATION_ROUTES.md)

Skill Checks (Optional):

Skill DC Result
HUMINT (Sense Motive vs Dmitri) 50 Success: Dmitri is genuinely trying to help (in his cold way) - not a setup
Navigate or Survival 40 Success: Understand Baltic conditions, can advise group on preparation
Craft (Boat) or similar 30 Success: Assess trawler is seaworthy (barely), but will survive trip

Boarding

Dmitri: "Get on boat. Hide below deck. I start engine in ten minutes. When we leave harbor, stay hidden. No lights, no sounds. Understand?"

Han går ombord och försvinner in i captain's cabin. Båten gungar i vågorna. Detta är verkligen the point of no return.

SAN Loss: 0/1 (ominous departure, dangerous crossing ahead, leaving NATO safety)

MINI-SCENE 2: SEA CROSSING

Duration: 36-48 hours at sea
Speltid: 45-60 minuter OR fast-forward
Mood: Cold, dark, nauseating, endurance test

Below Deck Life

Agenterna spenderar 36-48 timmar i a cramped compartment below deck:

Compartment Conditions

  • Size: 3m × 2m × 1.5m (barely room for 4 people)
  • Temperature: ~8-12°C (cold but above freezing)
  • Light: Small porthole (taped over for stealth), dim emergency light
  • Movement: Constant violent rocking from Baltic waves
  • Sound: Engine noise, waves crashing, wind howling
  • Supplies: 6 water bottles, dried fish, black bread, two wool blankets, bucket for waste

The First Night (Hour 0-12)

Båten lämnar Tallinn harbor i mörker. Dmitri kör utan lights, navigating by GPS and memory. Below deck, ni hör motor roar to life.

De första timmarna är worst: violent seasickness as boat hits open Baltic. Every wave feels like catastrophe.

Constitution × 5 Check: DC 40 or seasickness

  • Failure: Vomiting, -10% to all skills, lose 1d3 HP
  • Success: Nauseous but managing

Medicine or Survival Check: DC 30

  • Success: Help teammates avoid worst seasickness (+20% bonus to their CON checks)

Estonian Waters (Hour 12-24)

After 12 hours, ni är exhausted, cold, miserable. Then: engine cuts. Boat drifts.

Through hull, ni hör: another boat engine. Close. Very close.

Dmitri's voice (urgent whisper through vent): "Estonian patrol. Stay absolutely quiet."

GROUP STEALTH CHECK: All agents roll Stealth

DC Success Failure
30 Patrol passes without investigating Patrol hears movement, approaches boat (see Complication #3)

If Patrol Investigates:

  • Estonian Coast Guard boards boat, questions Dmitri
  • Dmitri's Deception: 60% - "Engine trouble, returning to port"
  • Guards search topside but don't check hidden compartment (unless agents failed VERY badly)
  • Adds 2-4 hours to journey

Russian Waters (Hour 24-36)

Andra natten. Ni är nu i Russian territorial waters. Dmitri becomes even more tense.

Through porthole (if you peek): Russian coast i distans. Lights from Sankt Petersburg glow in darkness.

Danger Escalates: Roll 1d100

Roll Encounter
01-20 Russian Patrol Boat: Dmitri must evade or bluff (see Optional Encounter #1)
21-40 Storm: Violent weather, CON × 3 check DC 50 or battered/injured (1d6 HP)
41-80 Tense but Uneventful: Dmitri navigates safely, arrive on schedule
81-100 Lucky: Fog rolls in, perfect cover, early arrival (-4 hours)

Constitution Endurance

After 36 hours at sea:

Constitution × 5 Check: DC 50 (brutal cold, exhaustion, cramped space)

  • Success: Exhausted (-10% physical skills) but functional
  • Failure: Hypothermia creeping in, lose 1d6 HP, -20% physical skills

SAN Loss: 0/1d3 (prolonged discomfort, fear of drowning, isolation)
