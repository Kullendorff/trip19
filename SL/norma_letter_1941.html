2406 16th Street N.W.
Washington, D.C.
March 17, 1941
Dear Harriet,
I am sending you these items Ernest wanted kept safe. I cannot bear to look at them. The FBI came again yesterday, asking the same questions about Ernest's contacts and his papers. They are polite, but persistent.
Please keep them away from the house. I do not know what they are or why he wanted them hidden, but he was most insistent in those final weeks. He said if anything happened, these should not be found with his other papers.
I trust you to keep them safe. Perhaps one day I will want to know what they mean, but not now. Not yet.
With gratitude,
Norma Lundeen
