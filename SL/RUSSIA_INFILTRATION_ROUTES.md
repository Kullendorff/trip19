# RUSSIA INFILTRATION ROUTES
## Moscow/Sankt Petersburg Entry Options via Character Contacts

**Campaign:** Trip 19 / Black Madonna - Chapter 5 Planning
**Status:** Outlaws operation - NO Delta Green handler support
**Time Pressure:** 2-5 days maximum preparation (NOT weeks)
**Geopolitical Context:** Russia 2025 = Ukraine war, extreme FSB surveillance, Western agents hunted

---

## CRITICAL CONSTRAINTS

### What Players CANNOT Do:
- ❌ Get Delta Green handler assistance (outlaws = no official support)
- ❌ Use FBI/Navy/NSA credentials (instant arrest in Russia 2025)
- ❌ Request weeks of prep time (urgency = days, not weeks)
- ❌ Expect extraction if caught (DG: "You're nobody. We can't get you back.")
- ❌ Enter via normal visa (extreme FSB surveillance + background checks)

### What Players CAN Do:
- ✅ Use personal contacts (bonds, relationships, old connections)
- ✅ Leverage Sparky's tech skills (24-48 hours darkweb access, encrypted comms)
- ✅ Use Scalpel's European family connections
- ✅ Pool money for smuggling payments ($30,000-$100,000 needed)
- ✅ Reactivate dormant contacts (risk to bonds + operational security)

## ROUTE 1: SPARKY'S DARKWEB HACKER ROUTE
### "Cipher's European Network"

**Contact Chain:**
1. **Sparky** → 2. **Cipher** (European hacker, 8-10 year friendship) → 3. **Darkweb smuggler markets** → 4. **Baltic smuggler networks**

### How Players Discover This Route:

**Scene: Sparky researching infiltration options**

Sparky mentions to the team: *"I know people online. Cipher's in Europe somewhere - we've never met face-to-face but we've worked together for almost a decade. Cryptography expert. If anyone knows how to move people quietly across borders..."*

**Investigation path:**
- Sparky contacts Cipher via encrypted channels (Signal, Tor network)
- Cipher asks: *"Why do you need this? This isn't like you."*
- Sparky must decide: Tell truth (Delta Green operation) or lie (personal emergency)
- **Bond erosion risk:** If Cipher discovers deception later, Bond damaged
- **Success:** Cipher provides contact to darkweb marketplace for "discrete travel services"

### The Route Details:

**Method:** Baltic Sea smuggling (Estonia → Sankt Petersburg)

**Logistics:**
- **Contact Point:** Tallinn, Estonia (EU territory, safer than Russia)
- **Smuggler Type:** Estonian/Russian mafiya contacts who run contraband across Gulf of Finland
- **Transport:** Fishing vessel or cargo ship (passengers hidden in containers/below deck)
- **Duration:** 3-4 days (1 day Tallinn prep, 1 day sea crossing, 1 day Sankt Petersburg arrival coordination)
- **Cost:** $100,000-$120,000 total (for 4-5 people)

**Process:**
1. **Day 1:** Sparky arranges via Cipher → Darkweb contact → Estonian smuggler rendezvous
2. **Day 1-2:** Fly to Tallinn (Scalpel uses Maya Nakamura identity, others use real passports)
3. **Day 2:** Meet smuggler contact in Tallinn (seedy bar, docks area)
4. **Day 2-3:** Board vessel, hidden transport across Gulf of Finland (200km sea crossing)
5. **Day 3-4:** Arrive Sankt Petersburg harbor, smuggled through FSB checkpoints

### Risks:

**HIGH RISK FACTORS:**
- 🔴 **FSB harbor patrols** - Coast guard active in Baltic after Poland incident
- 🔴 **Smuggler betrayal** - Mafiya could sell players to FSB ($$$)
- 🔴 **Weather delays** - Baltic storms in autumn/winter = days of delay
- 🔴 **Container inspection** - If harbor inspectors search cargo = instant arrest

**MEDIUM RISK FACTORS:**
- 🟡 **Cipher relationship damaged** - Using friend for dangerous operation
- 🟡 **Digital trail** - FSB monitors darkweb markets (Sparky's NSA skills help but not perfect)
- 🟡 **No backup plan** - If smuggler doesn't show up in Tallinn, stranded in Estonia

**Bond Consequences:**
- **Cipher (Sparky's Bond):** Potential erosion if operation goes wrong or Cipher learns full truth
- **Team cohesion:** High-stakes gamble on criminal smugglers = stress

### Why This Route Works:

✅ **Sparky has existing relationship** (8-10 years with Cipher)
✅ **Cipher is European** (timezone analysis suggests Western/Central Europe)
✅ **Hacker community knows darkweb markets** (this is realistic for their skillset)
✅ **Baltic smuggling is real** (Estonia-Russia border has active contraband trade)
✅ **Time: 3-4 days** (fits urgency constraint)

### GM Notes:

**Player agency:**
- Let Sparky roleplay conversation with Cipher (text-based, encrypted)
- Cipher can ask probing questions: *"Are you in trouble? Is this legal? Who are these people you're traveling with?"*
- Sparky must balance honesty (bond preservation) vs operational security (DG secrecy)

**Complications:**
- Smuggler contact could be FSB informant (10-15% chance)
- Sea crossing could encounter Russian patrol boats (Alertness rolls, potential combat)
- Team could get seasick, compromising readiness for Moscow operation

## ROUTE 2: SCALPEL'S BERLIN FATHER ROUTE
### "Jürgen's Bohemian Underground"

**Contact Chain:**
1. **Scalpel (Hanna)** → 2. **Jürgen Engler** (father, Berlin artist/musician, age 64) → 3. **Berlin art/music scene** → 4. **Eastern European tour connections** → 5. **Polish/Baltic smugglers**

### How Players Discover This Route:

**Scene: Team brainstorming, someone asks about European contacts**

Scalpel hesitates, then mentions: *"My father lives in Berlin. He's an artist... musician. Bohemian type. He knows people in... unconventional circles. I haven't told him I moved to Maryland yet."*

**Investigation path:**
- Scalpel calls Jürgen (first time in 4-6 months - bond maintenance roll?)
- Conversation in German (other players don't understand unless Sparky translates via app)
- Jürgen: *"Kleine Maus, is something wrong? You sound different."*
- Scalpel must ask father for help without revealing Delta Green

**Possible dialogue:**
- Scalpel: *"Vati, I need to get to Russia. Quietly. For work... medical research. But there are complications."*
- Jürgen: *"Hanna, this doesn't sound like medical research. Are you in danger?"*
- Scalpel: *"Can you help me or not?"*
- Jürgen (after pause): *"I know people. Not good people. But people."*

### The Route Details:

**Method:** Land route through Poland or Belarus

**Logistics:**
- **Contact Point:** Berlin, Germany (Jürgen's apartment in Kreuzberg)
- **Smuggler Type:** Eastern European art/music scene contacts who toured Soviet bloc during Cold War/90s
- **Route Options:**
  - **Option A (Poland):** Berlin → Warsaw → Białystok → Belarus border → Minsk → Russian border → Moscow (longer but avoids direct Russia entry)
  - **Option B (Direct):** Berlin → Polish border → Kaliningrad Oblast → Sankt Petersburg (shorter but higher FSB risk)

**Transport:** Van or truck (hidden in art equipment, musical instrument cases, or forged documentation)

**Duration:** 4-5 days
- Day 1: Berlin (meet Jürgen's contact)
- Day 2-3: Land travel (Poland/Belarus)
- Day 4: Border crossing (most dangerous part)
- Day 5: Arrival Moscow or Sankt Petersburg

**Cost:** $60,000-$80,000 (cheaper than sea route, but higher border risk)

### Risks:

**HIGH RISK FACTORS:**
- 🔴 **Polish-Russian border tensions** - After July 2025 incident, checkpoints extremely vigilant
- 🔴 **Belarus regime surveillance** - Lukashenko's government cooperates with FSB
- 🔴 **Forged documentation** - If papers don't hold up at checkpoint = arrest
- 🔴 **Jürgen's safety** - FSB could trace back to Berlin if operation exposed

**MEDIUM RISK FACTORS:**
- 🟡 **Father-daughter relationship strain** - Jürgen realizes Hanna is lying = bond damage
- 🟡 **Smuggler reliability** - Cold War-era contacts may be outdated (30 years old)
- 🟡 **Language barrier** - Only Scalpel speaks German (60%), rest of team isolated

**Bond Consequences:**
- **Jürgen Engler (Bond 8/12):** HIGH risk of erosion if he learns truth
- **Scalpel's guilt:** Using father for dangerous operation = psychological damage
- **Team trust:** Scalpel putting personal relationship at risk for mission

### Why This Route Works:

✅ **Scalpel has direct family contact** (father in Berlin)
✅ **Jürgen is bohemian artist** (historically connected to alternative/underground scenes)
✅ **Berlin to Eastern Europe pipeline** (musicians/artists toured Soviet bloc)
✅ **Scalpel speaks German** (60% skill, can navigate Berlin)
✅ **Time: 4-5 days** (fits urgency)

### GM Notes:

**Emotional weight:**
- This is Scalpel's FATHER - not just a contact
- Jürgen "accepts unconventional behavior" (per bond notes) but this crosses line
- Scalpel has avoided telling him about Maryland move (avoidance pattern)
- Using father for smuggling = potential bond break

**Father's reaction if truth discovered:**
- Jürgen is anti-authoritarian, artistic, open-minded
- Would be hurt by deception, not by danger
- *"Hanna, if you needed help, why didn't you just ask? Did you think I wouldn't understand?"*
- Potential for reconciliation OR permanent damage depending on player choices

**Complications:**
- Smuggler could be Stasi informant (still active from Cold War era)
- Border guards could demand bribes beyond initial payment
- Players could get separated during border crossing

## ROUTE 3: SPARKY'S "GHOST" MENTOR ROUTE
### "Reactivating the Disappeared"

**Contact Chain:**
1. **Sparky** → 2. **Marcus "Ghost" Reeves** (former hacktivist mentor, disappeared May 2018) → 3. **Underground hacktivist networks** → 4. **Anti-government smugglers** ("digital underground railroad")

### How Players Discover This Route:

**Scene: Sparky mentions a dangerous option**

After Route 1 discussion, Sparky says quietly: *"There's one other person. But I haven't talked to him in seven years. We separated for security. If I contact him now... it could burn everything he's built."*

**Investigation path:**
- Sparky must find Ghost's current contact method (he's VERY paranoid)
- Requires SIGINT rolls, darkweb archaeology, old code phrases
- **Risk:** Recontacting Ghost after 7 years = immediate suspicion
- Ghost's first response: *"Sparkplug? This better be life or death. You know the rules."*

**Sparky's dilemma:**
- Ghost taught her operational security, hacking ethics, activism philosophy
- Separated in 2018 to protect both of them (NSA employment = incompatible with hacktivism)
- Recontacting = admitting she needs his world again
- **Identity crisis:** Is she NSA analyst or hacktivist? Can't be both.

### The Route Details:

**Method:** "Underground railroad" for activists/dissidents escaping authoritarian governments

**Logistics:**
- **Contact Point:** Location unknown (Ghost will provide coordinates after vetting Sparky)
- **Network Type:** Anti-government activists who smuggle journalists, dissidents, whistleblowers across borders
- **Philosophy:** "Information wants to be free, people want to be free too"
- **Route:** Finland → Russia (grön gräns, "green border" forest crossing)

**Process:**
1. **Day 1:** Sparky recontacts Ghost, explains need (must pass his vetting)
2. **Day 2:** Ghost provides meeting location (likely European city - Amsterdam, Prague, Berlin)
3. **Day 2-3:** Team travels to meeting point
4. **Day 3:** Meet Ghost's contacts (hacktivist network cell)
5. **Day 4:** Travel to Finland-Russia border area
6. **Day 4-5:** Forest crossing with guides (night operation, 10-20km hike)

**Duration:** 4-5 days

**Cost:** $40,000-$60,000 (activists charge less than criminals, but still need funding)

### Risks:

**EXTREME RISK FACTORS:**
- 🔴🔴 **FSB border patrols** - Finland border heavily monitored (thermal imaging, drones, mines)
- 🔴🔴 **Ghost's paranoia** - If he thinks Sparky is compromised (NSA plant?) = refuses help OR ambushes
- 🔴🔴 **Wilderness survival** - Forest crossing in Russian winter = exposure, injury, death

**HIGH RISK FACTORS:**
- 🔴 **Activist network security** - If FSB infiltrated network = entire team arrested
- 🔴 **Physical demands** - 10-20km night hike with gear = Constitution checks
- 🔴 **Separation risk** - Forest crossing could scatter team

**MEDIUM RISK FACTORS:**
- 🟡 **Sparky's NSA employment** - Ghost may refuse to help "sellout"
- 🟡 **Activist ideology clash** - Team includes FBI agent (Mac), Navy chaplain (Sullivan) = ideological tension
- 🟡 **Future consequences** - Recontacting Ghost burns his network for Sparky forever

**Bond Consequences:**
- **Ghost (Not a bond, but relationship):** Recontacting after 7 years = trust broken OR renewed depending on honesty
- **Sparky's identity:** Choosing hacktivism over NSA loyalty = career risk
- **Team dynamics:** Mac (FBI) working with anti-government activists = moral tension

### Why This Route Works:

✅ **Sparky has history with Ghost** (mentor-mentee relationship, 2015-2018)
✅ **Hacktivist networks DO smuggle people** (whistleblowers, journalists, activists)
✅ **Ghost's paranoia = high operational security** (if he agrees, he'll be thorough)
✅ **Finland-Russia border crossable** (difficult but possible via forest)
✅ **Time: 4-5 days** (if Ghost agrees immediately)

### Why This Route Is Risky:

❌ **Ghost disappeared for security** (recontact = burning his new life)
❌ **Sparky doesn't know where Ghost is** (could take days just to find him)
❌ **Ideological clash** (team includes law enforcement = Ghost's enemies)
❌ **Physical danger** (wilderness crossing = actual survival skill checks)

### GM Notes:

**Ghost's character:**
- Age ~40-45 (was ~33-38 when mentoring Sparky)
- Radical hacktivist philosophy (anti-government, pro-transparency)
- Disappeared May 2018 when FBI raided Anonymous cells
- Would view Mac (FBI), Sullivan (military) with extreme suspicion
- Might refuse to help unless Sparky "proves" she's still loyal to the cause

**Moral dilemma for Sparky:**
- Ghost will ask: *"Why do you need this? What are you running from? Or running toward?"*
- If Sparky lies: Ghost's paranoia detects deception = refuses
- If Sparky tells truth (Delta Green): Ghost thinks she's insane OR compromised
- **Best option:** Partial truth ("Government conspiracy, can't explain, need to stop something bad")

**Team friction:**
- Mac will NOT be comfortable working with anti-government hacktivists
- Sullivan might object on moral grounds (smuggling is illegal)
- Scalpel would be pragmatic (outcome justifies means)
- **Potential scene:** Team argument about using "criminals" vs "activists"

**Complications:**
- Ghost could demand Sparky do something for the cause in exchange (hack NSA, leak documents)
- FSB could have moles in hacktivist network
- Forest crossing could encounter wild animals (wolves, bears)
- Weather could make crossing impossible

## ROUTE 4: COMBINED ROUTE - "THE BERLIN RENDEZVOUS"
### Using Multiple Contacts for Redundancy

**Concept:** Players don't rely on single route - they use Scalpel's Berlin father as base, then activate Sparky's darkweb OR Ghost contacts from Berlin

**Contact Chain:**
1. **Scalpel** → Jürgen (Berlin base) → 2. **Sparky** activates Cipher OR Ghost from Berlin → 3. Multiple smuggling options from central European hub

### How This Works:

**Step 1: Berlin Staging**
- Team flies to Berlin (Scalpel's father provides safe house)
- Jürgen's apartment becomes operational base
- Scalpel translates, provides local knowledge
- **Time:** 1 day

**Step 2: Parallel Contact Activation**
- Sparky contacts Cipher (darkweb route to Baltic)
- OR Sparky contacts Ghost (hacktivist route to Finland)
- **Advantage:** Two options if one fails
- **Time:** 1-2 days

**Step 3: Route Selection**
- Based on which contact responds first/most reliably
- Players choose Baltic (sea) or Finland (land) route
- Berlin → chosen route
- **Time:** 2-3 days

### Why This Is Smartest Option:

✅ **Redundancy** - Two routes means backup if one fails
✅ **Central European hub** - Berlin is well-connected to both Baltic and Finland
✅ **Scalpel's local knowledge** - German language, Berlin navigation
✅ **Operational security** - Multiple contacts = harder for FSB to track single pattern
✅ **Flexibility** - Can adjust based on which route opens fastest

### Risks:

**BOND STRAIN:**
- Using Jürgen's apartment for smuggling coordination = massive risk to father
- If FSB traces operation to Berlin → Jürgen could be arrested/interrogated
- Scalpel's relationship with father potentially destroyed

**OPERATIONAL COMPLEXITY:**
- Coordinating multiple contacts = more chances for mistakes
- Berlin attracts attention (Western intelligence active there)
- Team spending days in Berlin = more surveillance exposure

### Cost:

**Total:** $80,000-$120,000 depending on final route chosen
- Berlin safe house: $5,000-$10,000 (bribes, expenses)
- Route payment: $60,000-$100,000
- Contingency funds: $15,000-$20,000
