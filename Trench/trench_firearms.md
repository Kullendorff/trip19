## PENNSYLVANIA LICENSE TO CARRY FIREARMS (LTCF)

### Grundläggande Information
- **Typ:** Pennsylvania License To Carry Firearms (LTCF)
- **Status:** Aktiv (2012-nutid)
- **Utfärdare:** Philadelphia Sheriff's Office

## LEGAL COMPLIANCE & BEST PRACTICES

### Before Interstate Travel
1. **Kolla reciprocity map** - Säkerställ att destination-staten erkänner PA LTCF
2. **Läs destination state laws** - Var är det förbjudet att bära? (Skolor, government buildings, etc.)
3. **Plan route** - Undvik att köra genom New Jersey om beväpnad
4. **FOPA compliance** - Om måste passera genom hostile state: låst container, oladdat, ammo separerad

### During Operations
- **Know local laws** - Även om permit erkänns, följ DEN STATENS lagar
- **Concealed means concealed** - Dra inte uppmärksamhet
- **Avoid federal property** - Gun-Free School Zones Act gäller fortfarande
- **Vehicle transport** - Vissa stater kräver specifika storage requirements

### If Stopped by Police
- **Be polite and cooperative**
- **Inform officer immediately:** "I have a concealed carry permit and I'm armed"
- **Keep hands visible**
- **Show PA LTCF permit**
- **Do NOT reach for weapon**
- **Follow officer's instructions exactly**
